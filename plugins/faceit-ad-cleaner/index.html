BRIDGE PLUGIN · 1.0.0

FACEIT Ad Cleaner

Keep ad placements out of the page.

What it hides

FACEIT advertisement wrappers, Fuse ad slots and Google ad frames. The supplied post-match banner, its premium placeholder and its empty space are hidden together.

Always follows the page

New matching ads are hidden automatically as FACEIT changes pages or inserts content.

Turn it off

Disable this plugin in Bridge. Its stylesheet is removed and the placements become visible again.

This plugin changes visibility only. It does not block network requests or unlock Premium features. It requires style permission only and runs no page scripts.
